# Data Analytics & Visual Report

## Dataset Focus: Davao Region Agricultural Production Index (Mock CSV Analysis)

### 1. Data Cleaning Protocol Log

As part of the data preparation process, I uploaded the raw CSV dataset into an AI-assisted data analysis environment and performed a structural review of the database. During the inspection, I identified several data quality issues, including missing values, inconsistent numerical formats, duplicated entries, and mixed measurement units.

#### Structural Adjustments Performed

* Removed duplicate records and blank rows.
* Identified and replaced missing values in the Yield column using the median value of the corresponding crop category.
* Standardized all production measurements into Metric Tons (MT).
* Corrected inconsistent date and year formatting.
* Converted text-formatted numerical values into machine-readable numeric data types.
* Validated the dataset for outliers and formatting errors.

**Result:** The cleaned dataset was successfully normalized and prepared for visualization and trend analysis, improving overall data reliability and consistency.

### 3. Analytical Narrative

After reviewing the cleaned dataset and generated visualizations, I observed a notable decline in agricultural production during 2023, where output fell by approximately 17.5% compared to the previous year. While the AI-generated summary initially categorized this decline as a normal fluctuation in production levels, further contextual analysis suggests a stronger connection to environmental conditions affecting Mindanao during that period.

The decline coincides with the effects of the 2023 El Niño event, which brought prolonged dry conditions across several agricultural areas in Mindanao. Reduced rainfall likely affected crop productivity, particularly among smallholder farmers who rely heavily on rain-fed farming systems. This demonstrates that statistical trends should be interpreted alongside real-world environmental conditions to produce more accurate policy insights.

Based on these findings, I recommend that regional policymakers and LGUs prioritize investments in climate-resilient agricultural infrastructure, particularly solar-powered irrigation systems, water storage facilities, and farmer adaptation programs. Strengthening these areas can help reduce production losses during future climate-related disruptions and improve long-term food security within the region.

---

### Conclusion

Through AI-assisted data cleaning and visualization, I transformed a disorganized regional dataset into a structured analytical report capable of supporting evidence-based decision-making. The resulting charts reveal important production patterns and demonstrate how environmental factors can significantly influence regional economic performance. These insights may help guide future resource allocation and development planning initiatives in Mindanao.

# Mindanao Student Financial Literacy Prompt System

## 1. System Prompt Template (V3 – Final Optimized)

> Act as a Senior Financial Education Consultant specializing in the financial behavior of college students in the Davao Region. Your objective is to draft a 300-word financial literacy action plan for local universities and student organizations.

**Context:** Undergraduate students in Davao City are increasingly exposed to TikTok product promotions, online shopping platforms, and digital payment applications that may influence their spending behavior.

**Constraints:**

* Use a professional and student-friendly tone.
* Focus only on the Davao Region context.
* Do not discuss international stock markets or global economic issues.
* Avoid technical financial jargon.
* Include exactly three actionable recommendations.

**Format:** Output in Markdown and place the recommendations under the heading **"### Financial Literacy Recommendations"**.

### Conclusion

This prompt system demonstrates how localized prompt engineering can improve the relevance and quality of AI-generated outputs. By defining a specific audience, geographic context, tone, and formatting requirements, the system produces communication materials that are more applicable to financial literacy initiatives among college students in the Davao Region.
